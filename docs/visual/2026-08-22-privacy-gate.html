Privacy Gate

A model on your own machine strips the private parts before anything reaches the cloud. You approve exactly what leaves.

proposed, not built yet Track 3 · Hybrid UK AI Agent Lab · 22 Aug 2026

For: XreeceX, Elvin-Arrow

You have a bank statement you need help with. Today you either paste the whole thing into a cloud model and hope, or you get no help at all. Privacy Gate is the third option: a model running on your own laptop finds the sensitive bits, you tick what may be shared, and only that sanitised version is sent up.

Why anyone would want this

People constantly need help with documents they cannot hand over, payslips, medical letters, contracts, immigration paperwork, bank statements. The moment a document is sensitive, the useful tool becomes unusable. Most people either overshare without really knowing what they sent, or give up and do it by hand.

The interesting part is not the redaction. It is that the boundary itself is the product: the local model exists to make the cloud model safe to use.

How it works

Two models, one hard boundary. Nothing crosses the dashed line until you say so.

What that looks like on a real document

A payslip goes in. Gemma marks what it finds. You decide what survives the gate. proposed , all values below are invented

1 · on device

Gemma finds these:

Employee: A. Okafor NI number: QQ123456C Address: 14 Pelham St, SW7 Account: •••• 4417 Gross pay: £2,840.00 Tax paid: £412.60 Period: July 2026

2 · you choose

One tick per type, not per word:

blocked name · address · NI · account

shared pay figures · dates · employer type

In plain words: "share my income, hide who I am."

3 · what Gemini gets

Employee: [REDACTED] NI number: [REDACTED] Address: [REDACTED] Account: [REDACTED] Gross pay: £2,840.00 Tax paid: £412.60 Period: July 2026

Enough to reason with. Not enough to identify anyone.

Say this carefully. It is assisted redaction with human approval, never "guaranteed anonymisation". A judge may well push on this, and the honest framing is also the one that holds up: the user approves every field type before anything leaves.

Who builds what

Three parts, and they barely touch each other, so we can work in parallel from the start.

PartWhat it isTalks toHardest bit
Detector
local		 Regex baseline for the obvious things (account numbers, postcodes, NI numbers, emails), plus Gemma for names in context and free-text disclosure. Returns a list of {type, start, end}. Nothing. Pure function. Keeping Gemma's output short, a span map, never prose.
Consent UI Shows the document with spans highlighted. A checkbox per field type. Produces the redacted text and the audit entries. Takes spans, emits clean text. Making the redaction visibly happen on screen. This is the wow moment.
Reasoner
cloud		 Sends approved text to Gemini 3.7 Flash. Finds the inconsistency, explains it, drafts the reply. Takes clean text, returns findings. Not much, starter/ already has working examples.

Agree the shape of the span list in the first ten minutes and all three parts can be written without anyone waiting on anyone.

The two-minute demo

  1. Drop in a payslip and a bank statement. Pre-loaded, invented data.
  2. Gemma highlights the sensitive fields on screen, locally.
  3. Tick: share income, hide identity and account details.
  4. Show the sanitised payload that is about to be sent. This is the moment the audience understands the whole product.
  5. Gemini compares the two documents and finds the income figures do not match.
  6. It drafts the explanation letter.
  7. Open the audit log: what stayed local, what was shared.

Step 4 is the pitch. Everything else is setup for it.

Two things we already know

The local model is slow on the lead's laptop

observed

Measured on an M1 with 16GB: 10.8 tokens/sec on gemma4:e2b, and 4.7 tokens/sec on e4b which also takes 108 seconds just to load. It makes that machine sluggish while it is resident in memory.

So: run the local half on whichever machine is fastest, keep the model warm before demoing, and ask it for spans, never paragraphs.

What to cut if we run out of time

proposed

Keep: one document type, span detection, the consent tick-boxes, one Gemini task, the audit log.

Cut in this order: live screen capture → multi-document comparison → tool actions.

The audit log is cheap to build and it is the thing judges remember. Do not cut it.
