# 10 — Tracks, prizes, rules, rubric (ANNOUNCED 12:30, ground truth)

Organiser-published at the briefing. **Authoritative.** Overrides any inference in docs/08.

## Rubric — 100 points

| Weight | Criterion | What it actually asks |
|---|---|---|
| **30%** | Technical Execution & Model Leverage | How effectively you use Gemini and/or Gemma: multimodal streaming, tool use, speed, local inference, fine-tuning, efficiency |
| **25%** | Innovation & Originality | "More than a generic AI wrapper." A new workflow, interaction, or agentic experience |
| **25%** | Real-World Impact & UX | Solves a real problem, useful, clear and intuitive |
| **20%** | Presentation & Live Demo | Pitch quality, **demo reliability**, clarity of technical decisions, ability to defend in Q&A |

Note: model leverage is folded into the largest bucket. Using a feature deeply scores
better than using many features shallowly.

## Three tracks — each has its own top prize

Every track pays the same: **£400 cash + US$300 Gemini API credits per winner + GDM swag
+ continuing support from UK AI Agents Lab** if the team keeps building.

Three separate prizes means **track choice is a competitive decision**, not just a theme.
Pick the one where your build is strongest relative to who else will enter it.

### Track 1 — Most Creative Hack with Gemini 3.7 Flash
Cloud only. High-speed autonomous agents and interactive multimodal systems. Emphasis on
**low-latency streaming, dynamic tool use, structured outputs, complex reasoning**.

Organiser prompts: real-time multimodal workflows (video/audio assistants, live
vision-in-the-loop, screen copilots) · autonomous developer & CLI systems (agentic coding,
terminal debugging, infra operators, "inspired by G4TERM and Antigravity agent patterns")
· complex multi-document synthesizers (reason across diagrams, PDFs, webpages, live
streams into verifiable outputs).

### Track 2 — Best Use of Gemma
Local only. Privacy-preserving, offline-capable, or edge-optimised, on Gemma 4 open
weights on local hardware. Named runtimes: **LiteRT, Ollama, Transformers, vLLM**.

Organiser prompts: privacy-preserving clinical/domain tools (offline medical triage,
clinical note transcription, sensitive enterprise audit — no cloud egress) · edge hardware
& sensor agents (local controllers, robotics diagnostics on laptops, Raspberry Pi,
embedded) · offline education & civic utilities (classroom hubs, offline STEM tutors,
low-connectivity community tools).

### Track 3 — Best Hybrid AI & Human-Centric Utility
Gemini 3.7 Flash (cloud reasoning, multimodality, tool use) **combined with** Gemma 4
(local, private, edge). Stated interest: **social impact, education, accessibility,
creativity, human-centered AI**.

Organiser prompts: assistive & accessibility agents (convert visual environments into
audio or haptic guidance) · **"Sidewalk Safari"** — capture photos around Imperial and
reimagine them into stylized worlds, stories, interactive experiences · daily wellness &
public health companions (habits, nutrition from photos, actionable steps).

## Submission requirements — all four, by 17:30 GMT SHARP

1. **Public GitHub repository** — clean codebase, informative README.md, setup
   instructions, **architecture diagram**, **MIT or Apache license**.
2. **Proof of model integration** — code must explicitly use Gemini 3.7 Flash via the
   Google GenAI SDK / AI Studio, and/or Gemma 4 via local runtime or API.
3. **2-minute demo video** — Loom or YouTube. Working prototype, core user flow, what the
   product actually does.
4. **Short write-up, 2–3 paragraphs** — the problem, architecture choices, why Gemini
   and/or Gemma, future roadmap.

Four artefacts, each independently checkable. The architecture diagram and the licence
are the two most commonly forgotten and they cost nothing to produce.
